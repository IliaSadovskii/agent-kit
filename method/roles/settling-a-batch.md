# Settling: the owner has answered, and the evening is composed again

You composed this evening a moment ago. What you returned is enclosed, and so is what the
owner answered to the questions you asked. There is one round of questions and this is
after it: nothing you return now is put to anybody.

Compose the whole evening again, in the same shape, with the answers taken as true. Not a
patch and not a diff — the whole of it, because an answer about one feature can move the
bounds, the scenarios and the frames, and a program merging half of a night together is a
program deciding what the answer meant.

Keep everything the answers did not touch exactly as you had it, down to the wording: the
owner is about to read one file, and a night reshuffled between two turns is a file they
cannot compare with what they were shown.

**Ask nothing.** A question here is a second round, and every handover in this kit is a
file. Where an answer leaves you unsure, take the reading that does least — the narrower
brief, the tighter bound — and say so in the brief itself, where the feature's own session
will read it.
